## 1. Growth levers top creators credit (Recommendations, Notes, app, cross-promotion), plus Substack-published stats

### Takeaway
Substack's best-sourced official stat (Feb 2024) is that **Recommendations plus the Substack app drive about 50% of all new subscriptions and 25% of new paid subscriptions**. An earlier official post said "Substack generates 1 in 3 new subscriptions". Late-2025 figures such as "32M new subscribers from inside the app in 3 months" and "Notes = 25% of paid conversions" appear only in secondary sources. Creator anecdotes credit Notes, recommendations and live co-hosting.

### Cited Findings
- Official (Feb 2024): "Recommendations, along with the Substack app, powers a network that drives 50% of all new subscriptions and 25% of new paid subscriptions on Substack." Later pages repeat it as current, but it dates from early 2024. — [On Substack (cp/142007285)](https://on.substack.com/cp/142007285); [TechCrunch, Feb 22 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/22/substack-now-lets-writers-curate-a-network-of-recommended-publications-for-their-subscribers/amp/)
- An earlier official milestone post was titled "Substack generates 1 in 3 new subscriptions on the platform". Its date was not visible, but it likely predates 2024. — [On Substack](https://on.substack.com/p/substack-generates-1-in-3-new-subscriptions)
- Official posts on the Recommendations feature say writers reported that receiving recommendations accelerated their growth. — [On Substack: Recommendations update](https://on.substack.com/p/recommendations-update); [Introducing recommendations](https://on.substack.com/p/recommendations?open=false); [Turn on your growth engine](https://on.substack.com/p/turn-on-your-growth-engine)
- Secondary sources say Substack reported **32 million new subscribers from inside the app in three months**, attributed to an October 2025 creator event and described as "discovered via the in-app feed rather than external links". I did not find a primary Substack source. — [Blog Herald](https://blogherald.com/blog-platforms-tools/n-substack-reported-32-million-new-subscribers-from-inside-the-app-in-three-months-most-bloggers-are-missing-what-that-means/)

- Commentary argues that Substack's discovery algorithm favors writers who stay active inside the ecosystem (Notes, restacks, comments). — [Blog Herald](https://blogherald.com/blog/n-why-substacks-discovery-algorithm-favors-writers-who-stay-in-the-ecosystem/); [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/substack-rewards-ecosystem-activity-with-discovery-8fcfd144)

- Lenny Rachitsky credits consistent weekly publishing, guest contributions, an early viral Medium post and a VC friend's encouragement for his growth. — [On Substack: How Lenny Rachitsky earned $65,000](https://on.substack.com/p/how-lenny-rachitsky-earned-65000); [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/starting-a-business/how-this-newsletter-writer-got-more-than-300000/447172)
- A Mediabistro piece is headlined "LinkedIn drove 18 subscribers; here's what actually grew our newsletter", an example of external social channels underperforming. — [Mediabistro](https://www.mediabistro.com/mediabistro/linkedin-drove-18-subscribers-heres-what-actually-grew-our-newsletter/)
- Elle Griffin grew from 0 to 20,000 readers in one year and discusses "why social media didn't work" for her. — [Podcast episode](https://podbean.com/site/EpisodeDownload/DIR150E421EBNJRA)

### Inferences
- The defensible framing is that the in-network channels (app, Notes, Recommendations) account for a very large share of new subscriptions: officially about half of all new subscriptions as of 2024. External social media is often secondary for Substack-native growth.
- Recommendations favor writers who are already established. Small lists gain little from being recommended by other small lists.

### Gaps

- I could not retrieve Digiday's numbers for Live co-hosting or community tools.
- I found no Elle Griffin dataset on top-writer growth levers.

## 2. Free/paid mix, paywall strategy, pricing and conversion benchmarks

### Takeaway
There are two dominant models. **Mostly free with voluntary paid** (Heather Cox Richardson rarely paywalls but leads in paid revenue) and **heavy paywall plus bundled perks** (Lenny's Newsletter: one free post a month, Slack community, and a bundle of software subscriptions with the annual plan). Substack historically told writers to expect 5–10% conversion. Independent data puts typical conversion at about 2–5%, with a median around 3% and 1–2% for broad general-audience publications.

### Cited Findings
- **Platform scale:** In March 2025 Substack said it had passed **5 million paid subscriptions**, up from 2M in 2023. It raised a **$100M Series C at about $1.1B valuation** (July 17 2025), led by BOND and The Chernin Group. Chris Best said more than 50 people make over $1M a year on Substack. — [Axios Jul 17 2025](https://axios.com/2025/07/17/substack-newsletter-funding-creator-economy); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/17/substack-raises-100m-from-chernin-group-andreessen-horowitz-skims-ceo-and-more/); [Variety](https://variety.com/2025/digital/news/substack-raises-100-million-chernin-group-skims-investors-1236463721); [American Bazaar](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2025/07/18/substack-raises-100-million-in-series-c-to-grow-creator-subscription-platform-465236/)
- Unverified secondary figures: Substack commission revenue of about $45–48M in 2025; 35M–50M+ total active subscriptions in 2026 (the figures conflict); UK paid subscriptions above 500,000 (May 2026). — [demandsage](https://demandsage.com/substack-statistics/); [bestwriting](https://bestwriting.com/substack-statistics); [pulse.bot UK](https://www.pulse.bot/media/news/substack-uk-paid-subscriptions-top-500000-signaling-creator-economy-surge-de83ac48-7342-433e-b739-230926a682b0/)
- **Lenny's Newsletter:**
  - He launched the paid tier in April 2020, about nine months after starting. After one month he had 13,000 free and 486 paid subscribers (about $56K gross ARR).
  - By the end of 2020 he had about 45,000 free and 3,300 paid subscribers (about $360K ARR). — [On Substack](https://on.substack.com/p/how-lenny-rachitsky-earned-65000); [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/starting-a-business/how-this-newsletter-writer-got-more-than-300000/447172)
  - Pricing is $15/month, $150/year, and a $300 higher tier. Paid subscribers get the full weekly post and a Slack community. Free subscribers get one free post a month. — [Newsletter Circle: 8 paywalling strategies](https://newslettercircle.substack.com/p/8-proven-paywalling-strategies-used); [command.ai](https://command.ai/blog/lenny-rachitsky-newsletter-growth)

  - Revenue estimates are third-party only and range from Latka's "Gross $2.7M+" with 18,000+ paid subscribers to about $916K. Lenny: "I don't share my revenue." — [Latka](https://getlatka.com/blog/lennys-newsletter-arr); [Ark (beehiiv)](https://ark.beehiiv.com/p/how-lenny-rachitsky-makes-500000-year-with-a-newsletter)

- **Heather Cox Richardson (Letters from an American):**
  - She had over 2.6M subscribers as of July 2025 and more than 2.7M by November 2025. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heather_Cox_Richardson)

- **Paywall placement:** Substack supports mid-post paywall embeds, so a post can be free up to a point and paid after it. — [On Substack: new tools / paywall embeds](https://on.substack.com/p/new-tools-paywall-embeds/comment/3384439)

### Inferences
- A paywall works best when it comes with a reason to pay: perks, community, archive access, or a deep-dive vs. summary split (Lenny). Mission or patronage loyalty works for very large free audiences (HCR).
- For planning, assume about 2–3% conversion. Treat 5–10% as an upper bound for niche, high-value professional content.

### Gaps
- Substack has published no official founding-tier conversion data and no current churn benchmarks.
- I could not confirm whether Lenny's archive is fully paid-only as of 2026. The sources describe "one free post a month" but may be outdated.

## 3. Publishing cadence and send timing: what the data says

### Takeaway
There is no rigorous public study showing a universal best send time for Substack emails. Substack staff once noted weekends were slightly higher (Sunday above Saturday). A 473K-Notes study found only about a 3-point spread between the best and worst posting hour for Notes. Consistency beats timing, and top creators keep a fixed weekly rhythm.

### Cited Findings
- A Substack staffer (informal comment): "generally, open rates are slightly higher on weekends, and Sundays are better than Saturdays." — [On Substack office hours comment](https://on.substack.com/p/new-tools-paywall-embeds/comment/3384439)
- Eden.so analyzed 473,405 Notes. Early morning US Eastern and Mondays were slightly ahead, but "the entire gap between the best and worst posting hour is about 3 percentage points". The study covers Notes only, not email. — [Eden.so](https://eden.so/blog/best-time-to-post-on-substack/)
- In one creator's own test, open rates were about 40% "regardless of the day". — [Wander Wealth](https://wanderwealth.substack.com/p/whens-the-best-time-to-post-your)
- Unverified blog claims favor Tuesday 9–11am or Tuesday to Thursday 7–9am local time. — [emaillistvalidation](https://emaillistvalidation.com/blog/best-days-times-publish-substack-growth/); [substackcourse](https://substackcourse.com/best-times-to-post-on-substack)
- Notes timing analysis based on 71 Notes. — [Alesia Zakharova](https://alesiazakharova.substack.com/p/i-analyzed-71-substack-notes-over)
- Lenny attributes growth to consistent weekly publishing. — [On Substack](https://on.substack.com/p/how-lenny-rachitsky-earned-65000)
- Garbage Day satirizes optimal-timing advice ("This subject line is the optimal…"). — [Garbage Day](https://garbageday.email/p/this-subject-line-is-the-optimal)

### Inferences
- Practical rule: choose a fixed weekly day and time and A/B test over 4 or more weeks using your own Substack stats. Post Notes daily or several times a day. The hour matters little.
- HCR publishes nearly daily, and Lenny publishes weekly plus podcast episodes. Cadence follows the format (news-commentary vs. deep dives).

### Gaps
- Substack has published no official send-time study.

## 4. Metrics creators track and how they iterate

### Takeaway
The native dashboard covers subscribers (free and paid), open rate, views, traffic sources and conversions. As of March 2026 stats can be exported to CSV. Creators track open rate, free-to-paid conversion, subscriber sources (app/Notes/recommendations vs. external) and Notes engagement, and they iterate on formats such as Note types.

### Cited Findings
- Substack added stats CSV exports in March 2026. — [AlternativeTo, Mar 2026](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/3/substack-adds-draft-notes-multi-post-pinning-stats-csv-exports-and-live-video-management)

- Critique of attribution blind spots: "What Substack Doesn't Tell You About Your Subscribers". — [Kim Doyal](https://kimdoyal.substack.com/p/what-substack-doesnt-tell-you-about)
- Third-party analytics tools: Finntropy's dashboard ($20/month; "What 13,844 newsletters reveal") and Stacksweller (Chrome extension). — [Finntropy](https://finntropy.substack.com/p/what-13844-newsletters-reveal-about?open=false); [Finntropy dashboard](https://finntropy.gumroad.com/l/substack_dashboard); [Stacksweller](https://chromeboard.com/extension/stacksweller-pakfbmkpimmfpeadnbgoeoieiechhapb)

### Inferences

## 5. Repurposing and distribution workflows (Notes, threads, podcast, video clips, social, SEO, referrals, welcome email)

### Takeaway
Substack is building repurposing into the product. The Recording Studio (Mar 2026) auto-generates promo clips, livestreams can be co-hosted and scheduled, and Notes can be drafted and scheduled (Mar–Apr 2026). Built-in growth tools include cross-posting, Recommendations, three-tier referral rewards and welcome-email customization.

### Cited Findings
- **Recording Studio (Mar 2026):** pre-record solo or multi-guest shows, screen sharing, overlays, and auto-generated promotional clips. — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/3/substack-launches-a-new-recording-studio-tool-for-pre-recorded-shows-and-new-overlay-tools); [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/feed/substack-launches-built-in-recording-studio-for-video-creators)
- **Notes workflow:** draft Notes and multi-post pinning (Mar 2026); Note scheduling and post templates (Apr 2026). — [AlternativeTo Mar](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/3/substack-adds-draft-notes-multi-post-pinning-stats-csv-exports-and-live-video-management); [AlternativeTo Apr](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/4/substack-adds-note-scheduling-post-templates-drop-caps-new-livestream-options-and-more)
- **Cross-posting:** "tap the cross-post button, then write a blurb… Your cross-post will be sent via email." — [On Substack product dispatch](https://on.substack.com/p/product-news-dispatch-nov-22)
- **Referral program:** rewards can be set at three tiers, and Substack automatically emails subscribers when they unlock one. A common pattern is a 1-month comp for 3 referrals, 6 months for 10, and 12 months for 25. — [On Substack: subscriber referrals](https://on.substack.com/p/subscriber-referrals); [Rachelle Robinett example](https://rachellerobinett.substack.com/p/rewards-for-referralsinvite-your)
- **Welcome email, headers and footers:** Substack calls them "often overlooked growth tools". — [On Substack: subscriber referrals](https://on.substack.com/p/subscriber-referrals)
- Third-party advice: newsletter swaps, and launching referrals once a list passes about 500 subscribers (unsourced impact figures). — [Supascribe](https://supascribe.com/blog/how-to-grow-substack-newsletter)
- SparkLoop (vendor, likely dated) called Substack "the worst email platform for running a referral program" because of its integration limits. — [SparkLoop](https://sparkloop.app/blog/referral-program-substack-9)
- **SEO:** recipe embeds (Feb 2026) auto-generate metadata for search visibility. — [AlternativeTo Feb 2026](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/2/substack-brings-improved-design-editing-recipe-embeds-and-expanded-livestream-scheduling)
- **Podcast model:** Lenny's growth to "1M+ subscribers and a top-10 tech podcast" pairs the newsletter with a podcast. — [video summary (pulse.bot)](https://www.pulse.bot/marketing/video/how-i-built-a-1m-subscriber-newsletter-and-top-10-tech-podcast-lenny-rachitsky-21ca7f9d-0350-42df-b151-ea54600a5fa0)

### Inferences
- A typical workflow: long post, then 3–10 Notes (quotes, charts, hooks) scheduled across the week, then a Live session or Recording Studio episode, then auto-clips for Notes and external platforms (YouTube Shorts, X, LinkedIn, Threads).
- Cross-posts and Live co-hosts work as low-cost collaborations that put each writer in front of the other's audience.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve specific creator SOPs for repurposing to X, LinkedIn or Threads, or YouTube distribution data.

## 6. Current Substack platform features and policies (2025–2026), including AI, app, video, live, and API/automation

### Takeaway
2025–2026 releases centered on video and live: Recording Studio, a live video management hub, livestream scheduling and stream keys, TV apps, private Substacks, and Chat updates. In July 2026 Substack introduced Pangram-based AI detection, a bet that readers will pay for human writing. There is **no official posting/write API**. The official Developer API is narrow (profile lookup), and automation relies on RSS, import tools, or fragile unofficial APIs.

### Cited Findings
- **Video and live:**
  - Recording Studio (Mar 2026). — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/3/substack-launches-a-new-recording-studio-tool-for-pre-recorded-shows-and-new-overlay-tools)
  - Live video management section, centralizing scheduled streams, recordings and drafts (Mar 2026). — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/3/substack-adds-draft-notes-multi-post-pinning-stats-csv-exports-and-live-video-management)
  - Stream-key access for contributors, admins, owners and hosts, plus new livestream options (Apr 2026). — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/4/substack-adds-note-scheduling-post-templates-drop-caps-new-livestream-options-and-more)

- **TV apps (Jan 2026):** support free and paid subscribers. Audio and more discovery features are on the roadmap. Writer reaction was mixed. — [rudix mirror article](https://monorepo.rudix.workers.dev/article/6645175)
- **Private Substacks and Chat updates.** Exact date unclear. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2475815)
- **Other mentioned features (secondary podcast source):** native sponsorships, Creator Kits, subscriber perks, QR codes. — [Spreaker episode](https://www.spreaker.com/episode/new-substack-features-every-creator-should-know-309--75041690)
- **AI policy:**
  - Axios (Jul 23 2026): "Substack bets readers want to pay for content written by humans." The piece covers Pangram-based detection that gives a percentage AI vs. human score, which drew creator backlash ("witch hunt"). — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/23/substack-subscribers-ai-generated-content-pangram); [Artiverse](https://www.artiverse.ca/substacks-ai-detection-sparks-backlash-and-debate-on-transparency/)
  - A French headline says Substack bans AI-generated newsletters. Unconfirmed. — [munci.org](https://www.munci.org/substack-bannit-les-newsletters-generees-par-lia/)

  - Terms of Use prohibit scraping and crawling, and Substack does not guarantee the accuracy or copyright compliance of AI-generated content. — [ConductAtlas ToS provision](https://conductatlas.com/platform/substack/substack-terms-of-use/provision/CA-P-029302/prohibition-on-scraping-or-crawling-substack/); [ToS diff](https://conductatlas.com/diff/substack/substack-terms-of-use/4272/7372/)

- **API and automation:**
  - There is no official API for creating drafts, publishing posts or managing subscribers, and no official SDKs.
  - The official Developer API, released early or April 2026 (sources differ), returns public creator profile data for a LinkedIn handle. It requires an application and acceptance of the API Terms of Use (updated Jan 8 2026).
  - A first-party read-only MCP endpoint (July 2026, limited to Bestseller publications and admins) is reported by one source. It conflicts with another report that the CEO said in July 2026 that MCP was "coming soon".
  - The RSS feed (`/feed`) is the most stable read path. — [dev.to: Does Substack have an API (2026)](https://dev.to/equinoxaifinancergb/does-substack-have-an-api-2026-and-what-you-can-actually-get-29il); [apis.io](https://apis.io/providers/substack/); [API Evangelist](https://providers.apievangelist.com/providers/substack/)
  - Unofficial "Substack posting API" tools use reverse-engineered endpoints. They are fragile and may violate the ToS. — [aiindigo review](https://aiindigo.com/blog/review-unofficial-substack-posting-api-2026-edition); [aiindigo tool](https://aiindigo.com/tool/i-updated-my-unofficial-substack-posting-api)
  - Third-party scrapers such as the Apify Notes scraper exist but are subject to the ToS. — [Apify](https://apify.com/brick_joey_yto/substack-notes-scraper)
  - Claude–Substack integration guide (third party). — [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/claude-substack-integration)

### Inferences
- An agent-based publishing workflow should assume: drafting outside Substack, then manual paste or the official importer. Analytics come from CSV exports. Notes scheduling is native but not API-accessible. Unofficial write APIs carry account risk.
- With AI detection live since mid-2026, heavily AI-written posts risk reader-trust and labeling penalties. AI is safer for research, outlining and repurposing than for final prose.

- I could not verify the RSS/WordPress/Medium import feature details from primary sources in this session. Substack has long supported importing posts and mailing lists, but that comes from background knowledge and was not verified here.
- No Substack-published 2026 usage stats were found for Live, video or Chat.
