## 1. Topic selection (evergreen vs trending, tags/topics, what earns Boosts)

### Takeaway
Medium's own guidance points writers to topics they can cover **from personal experience or expertise**, and to stories that are "original" and "enrich the reader's life." Since 2023 that has been the practical route to Boosts and to member reading time. Top writers reach the same place from another direction. Evergreen self-improvement, money and writing topics, told through personal stories, made up the core of the most profitable catalogs (Denning, Awosika, Foroux, Ashley). Trend-chasing and "how to grow on Medium" meta-posts are increasingly criticized, by Kuegler among others.

### Cited Findings
- Medium's Distribution Guidelines (help page, updated June 29, 2026 per snippet) list "AI-generated content" with generic or derivative pieces as **low-value content** under General Distribution. Their examples praise content "based directly on the writer's experience." Republished translations are not eligible for Boost. — [Medium Help: Distribution Guidelines](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006362473-Medium-s-Distribution-Guidelines-How-curators-review-stories-for-Boost-General-and-Network-Distribution)
- Medium's writer guidance says "readers highly value personal expertise and insight, so that's what we look for." Boosted stories should "move readers forward, helping them deepen their understanding of themselves, others, their endeavors, or the world." — [Medium Help (Aug 14, 2024 version)](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006362473)
- Boost criteria were revised. Older questions ("Is it constructive?", "Does it feel memorable?") were dropped after complaints ("This is a humor piece, it isn't meant to be constructive!"). Curators now ask whether "the reader's life [is] enriched by reading the story" and whether it is "original, human-created content… explores something previously unknown or not frequently examined." — [Medium Blog: Updated Guidelines for Boost](https://medium.com/blog/updated-guidelines-for-boost-47799aad8899)
- The earlier pillars, as summarized by third parties, were: constructive, original, written from experience, well-crafted, memorable. Medium's CEO said Boosted stories should be thought of "as if they are cover stories for a magazine." — [Linda C. Smith (editor) on Substack](https://lindac.substack.com/p/some-thoughts-on-mediums-new-boost)
- Medium's Writer Newsletter published prompts (e.g., "10 writing prompts to start 2026"), which is official nudging toward topic ideas. — [Medium Handbook: Writer Newsletter](https://medium.com/medium-handbook/writer-newsletter-10-writing-prompts-to-start-2026-b451afade41a)
- Tags: up to five tags can be added at publish time, which "will help your story reach more readers within Medium itself." Choosing closely aligned topics means Medium shows the piece to readers interested in them. This is third-party advice. — [Medium Context: How to Get Your Content Read](https://medium.com/context/how-to-get-your-content-read-on-medium-53a6e44833af)
- Shannon Ashley earned steadily with vulnerable personal pieces on mental health, relationships and personal growth. — [Pen & Pixel guide](https://medium.com/pen-pixel/how-to-earn-on-medium-a-comprehensive-guide-bc6d0b4220aa)
- Darius Foroux covers wealth, habits and decision-making, which are evergreen productivity and money niches. — [Pen & Pixel guide](https://medium.com/pen-pixel/how-to-earn-on-medium-a-comprehensive-guide-bc6d0b4220aa); [Foroux: 6 Steps to Get Rich as a Writer](https://dariusforoux.com/rich-writer/)
- Tim Denning's method, as read by a third party, "recycles" his own topics by revisiting them from fresh angles. He does not keep hunting for new topics. — [Burk: How to write Medium articles that earn money](https://blog.byburk.net/how-to-write-medium-articles-that-earn-money)
- Tom Kuegler, Substack Note: Medium has "WAY TOO MANY 'how to grow on Medium' meta posts that ruin the platform." He also wrote: "Nobody has the idea that you can get rich on Substack by going viral. You can only do that on Medium. So everyone just shoots to write the viral crap." — [Kuegler Note 1](https://substack.com/@tomkuegler/note/c-51203429); [Kuegler Note 2](https://substack.com/@tomkuegler/note/c-51592384)
- Medium staffer Zulie Rane (writing as "Zulie @ Medium") keeps a curated list explaining Boost and a post on "Stories that almost get Boosted." The post's content could not be fetched. — [Zulie @ Medium: Everything about the Boost](https://medium.com/@zulie_at_medium/list/everything-about-the-boost-aea02a8f55f9); [Stories that almost get Boosted](https://medium.com/@zulie_at_medium/stories-that-almost-get-boosted-99be07d01779)

### Inferences
- Under reading-time-weighted pay plus Boost, the best expected-value topic is **evergreen, experience-based, in a niche with many paying members** (self-improvement, money, writing, tech career). Trending news scores poorly on the "original/experience" Boost test.
- Repeating topics, as Denning does, is a deliberate efficiency strategy. Proven themes are re-angled, not abandoned.

### Gaps
- I could not read Medium's topic pages or the "Stories that almost get Boosted" text. No primary data on which topics earn the most Boosts.
- No source found on reader-search or keyword tooling inside Medium. Medium earns from search-engine discovery (see §5), but no writer process for SEO keyword research was documented.

## 2. Article structure, headlines, subtitles, images, length, formatting

### Takeaway
Nicolas Cole's frameworks are the most fully documented. His headline answers **what / who / promise**. He drafts the headline first. Articles follow intro + about 3 points + conclusion, and paragraphs follow a 1/3/1 sentence rhythm. Medium's official stance is that the **title, subtitle and cover image must give enough context**, or the story will not be Boosted. Length should "serve the purpose." No official length target exists. A 5–7 minute read is community folklore.

### Cited Findings
- **Cole's headline formula:** a headline answers three questions: what the piece is about, who it is for, and what it promises. — [Nicolas Cole on Medium: 3 Simple Ways to Make Your Medium Headlines Irresistible](https://nicolascole77.medium.com/3-simple-ways-to-make-your-medium-headlines-irresistible-click-6a401cc0a02b); [ILLUMINATION: Answer These 3 Questions](https://medium.com/illumination/answer-these-3-questions-to-write-great-titles-4e09ff088eab)
- Cole recommends writing the headline **before** the article so the draft has direction. — [Technical Excellence: Steal Nicolas Cole's ideas](https://medium.com/technical-excellence/steal-nicolas-coles-ideas-for-writing-irresistible-headlines-that-work-1a43d886ce6b)
- Cole's headline formats (from *The Art and Business of Online Writing*): Big Numbers, Dollar Signs, Credible Names, question/answer, "X number of…" lists. — [Evolve: 10 Proven Headline Formats by Nicolas Cole](https://medium.com/evolve/10-proven-headline-formats-by-nicolas-cole-that-will-get-you-thousands-of-views-b88dfa8581f3); [StoryLab summary](https://storylab.ai/the-art-and-business-of-online-writing-nicolas-cole/)
- **Cole's article skeleton:** introduction, X main points, strong conclusion. About 3 main points is the usual maximum unless the piece is a listicle. **Paragraph rhythm** follows sentence-count patterns such as 1/3/1, 1/5/1 and 1/1/2/1. — [Teesche book review](https://www.teesche.com/bookshelf/nicolas_cole_the_art_and_business_of_online_writing); [Fastlane forum summary](https://www.thefastlaneforum.com/community/threads/book-summary-the-art-and-business-of-online-writing-by-nicolas-cole.99943/)
- Cole's "How To" business formula (Substack Note): "Want to solve X? Follow 1-2-3." Name the problem, pinpoint actionable steps, celebrate the outcome. — [Nicolas Cole Substack Note](https://substack.com/@nicolascole77/note/c-255086916)
- **Medium official:** the title, subtitle and cover image "are what we present on Medium, and are what readers see when deciding whether or not to read your story." Medium "will not Boost a story if we don't believe the title, subtitle, and cover image provide enough context." The story should be "well-written, free of errors, appropriately sourced, and narratively strong." "Correct formatting is one of many indicators of good craftsmanship." Images should add value and carry alt text and credits. Length should be "appropriate and [serve] the purpose and context of the story itself." — [Medium Help: Distribution Guidelines](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006362473-Medium-s-Distribution-Guidelines-How-curators-review-stories-for-Boost-General-and-Network-Distribution)
- Subtitles are set in the editor by highlighting the line under the title and applying subtitle format. Display title and subtitle can be customized separately for search and social previews. — [DEV: Boost your Medium visibility](https://dev.to/ssukhpinder/boost-your-medium-visibility-12-essential-tips-for-writers-2883)
- Community claims, not official: 5–7 minute reads (800–1,200 words) "perform best." Use white space, lists and subheadings. Aim for about a 70% read ratio. — [Medium Context](https://medium.com/context/how-to-get-your-content-read-on-medium-53a6e44833af) and similar third-party posts in the same search. Flagged as anecdotal.
- Awosika's drill: write 10 new headlines a day at the start of each writing session, and read 3–5 posts from top writers daily. — [Awosika course/sales material](https://ayodeji-s-awosika.mykajabi.com/medium-writing-superstars) (promotional source)

### Inferences
- Because pay depends on member reading time and engagement (claps, highlights, replies), structure that keeps readers to the end matters more than a high click-through headline. That favors Cole-style scannable formatting with a clear promise that the piece delivers.
- The rule that the title, subtitle and image must give context works as an official anti-clickbait filter. Curiosity-gap headlines with no context risk losing the Boost.

### Gaps
- No official Medium statement on optimal read time. The "5–7 min" figure is folklore.
- Could not retrieve Medium's own featured-image or style guide pages in full.

## 3. Drafting/editing, volume and cadence, publications/editors, AI policy

### Takeaway
Output volume historically ran very high. Denning is reported at 8–10 pieces a week across platforms, and Shannon Ashley at about 3 a day per one source. Zulie Rane, by contrast, earned well on 2–6 stories a month. Publications matter because **Boost works through nomination**: editors of Boost-enabled publications nominate stories and Medium curators decide. Since June 2026, editors themselves earn through an Editor Partner Program. AI writing is allowed but **cannot be paywalled** (since May 1, 2024) and is not eligible for Boost. Undisclosed AI writing gets Network-Only distribution.

### Cited Findings
- **Denning's cadence:** a profile says 10 articles a week across Medium, LinkedIn and his personal site. Another post says 8 a week for 5 years, with 37 stories in one month. A third says "two high-quality articles per day for several years." These figures conflict. — [yespress profile](https://yespress.io/tim-denning); [readmedium: How often does Tim Denning publish](https://readmedium.com/how-often-does-tim-denning-publish-stories-on-medium-8fe972469230); [Feedium: Stop complaining](https://medium.com/feedium/new-writers-on-medium-stop-complaining-that-youre-not-making-big-money-on-medium-yet-eabb0247c8b7)
- Shannon Ashley is said to write "3 times a day." This comes from a third-party snippet and is unverified. — [Pen & Pixel guide](https://medium.com/pen-pixel/how-to-earn-on-medium-a-comprehensive-guide-bc6d0b4220aa)
- Zulie Rane (2023) typically earned $1,500–$3,000 a month from **2 to 6 stories a month**. — [Zulie Writes: How much do Medium writers make](https://www.zuliewrites.com/blog/how-much-do-medium-writers-make); [Zulie Writes: Is Medium still worth it in 2023](https://www.zuliewrites.com/blog/is-medium-still-worth-it-in-2023)
- **Boost Nomination Program:** "how independent publication editors nominate high-quality stories for broader distribution across Medium." Medium curators make the final call, usually within 1 business day (sometimes 2). English only. The program was not accepting new publication applications at the time of the help page. — [Medium Blog: BNP editors](https://blog.medium.com/bnp-editors-9c0a6f5a133a); [Help: Boost Nomination Program Participation](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/26729526741399-Boost-Nomination-Program-Participation); [Help: What happens after I nominate](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/26716468567959-What-happens-after-I-nominate-a-story-for-Boosting)
- A Boost-enabled editor's view: "Medium Secrets Only A Boost-Enabled Publication Editor Will Know." Content not retrieved. — [Anangsha Alammyan](https://www.anangsha.me/medium-secrets-only-a-boost-enabled-publication-editor-will-know/)
- **June 1, 2026:** Medium launched an Editor Partner Program that pays editors for stories they edit. Medium says writers will not see a drop because it increased the total subscriber funds paid out. — [Medium Blog: Starting June 1, rewarding editors](https://medium.com/blog/partner-program-update-starting-june-1-were-rewarding-editors-for-stories-they-edit-aaec12a97cea)
- **AI policy:** from May 1, 2024, "AI-generated writing, whether disclosed or not, will no longer be eligible for paywalling under the Partner Program." Accounts with AI content behind the paywall risk losing paywall status or Partner Program enrollment. "Medium is for human storytelling, not AI-generated writing." — [Plagiarism Today, Apr 11 2024](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2024/04/11/medium-sets-new-policies-on-ai-generated-writing/); [AlternativeTo, Apr 2024](https://alternativeto.net/news/2024/4/medium-bans-ai-generated-content-from-its-partner-program-upholding-human-storytelling); [MediaNama, Apr 2024](https://www.medianama.com/2024/04/223-medium-ai-transparency-bars-paywalled-ai-content/)
- **AI distribution tiers:** disclosed AI writing that is not paywalled is eligible for General Distribution but not Boost. Undisclosed AI writing gets "Network Only" distribution (followers and subscribers only). Medium says "there is no 100% foolproof method of detecting AI, and we don't take any automatic actions against detected or suspected AI content." Publications may set stricter rules. — [Medium Help: Policy on AI Writing](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/22223403883287-Medium-s-Policy-on-Artificial-Intelligence-AI-Writing); [Plain English: How we're approaching AI-generated writing](https://ai.plainenglish.io/how-were-approaching-ai-generated-writing-on-medium-a-human-perspective-cae3cef31f88?gi=6bb20c1d4921)
- AI "slop" is cited by writers as degrading Medium. — [Agileway: How does AI slop affect Medium writers](https://agileway.substack.com/p/how-does-ai-slop-affect-medium-writers)

### Inferences
- Under the current model (Boost plus engagement weighting plus editor pay), the incentive has shifted from **volume** (the 2018–2022 Denning era) toward **fewer, edited, Boost-worthy pieces submitted through Boost-enabled publications**.
- AI is safe to use only as an assistant (research, outlining, editing). Paywalled stories must be human-written. No detection is automatic, but curator judgment of "human-created" is part of Boost review.

### Gaps
- No first-hand drafting or editing workflows were found for Denning, Göke, Michael Thompson, Sinem Günel or Benjamin Hardy (searches returned nothing usable).
- It is unclear whether *light* AI assistance (grammar, outline), when disclosed, is allowed behind the paywall. Reports describe this as ambiguous ([MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2024/05/223-medium-detect-ai-content-new-policy/)).
- No Medium statement covered tech publications such as Better Programming's editorial process.

## 4. Repurposing and Medium's role in the funnel

### Takeaway
Nearly every top writer describes Medium as a **discovery and income vehicle feeding an owned list**. They do not treat it as the destination. Awosika turned Medium into a 35k email list that drove $1M in course sales. Denning moved to Substack ("if you don't own your audience, you don't own anything"). Kuegler left Medium in Aug 2023 for Substack. Towards Data Science, Medium's largest publication, went independent in Feb 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **Awosika:** "I've been writing on Medium for seven years, made $440k from the platform" and used it to build a 35,000-person email list. The list produced about $100K in book royalties and about $1M in course sales. Elsewhere he says he moved to Substack after Medium earnings fell about 90%. — [Awosika LinkedIn post (~Jan 2023)](https://lt.linkedin.com/posts/ayotheauthor_ive-been-writing-on-medium-for-seven-years-activity-7020457143103328256-a4hb); [Awosika LinkedIn ($147 → $1M)](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ayotheauthor_i-went-from-147-in-my-bank-account-to-making-activity-7172970225410129920-ZLDw)
- **Denning:** platform money "can be taken off you at any time." He urges writers to build an email list ("if you don't own your audience, you don't own anything," quoted by a commentator). When he left Medium he reportedly lost about 326,000 followers. His Substack bio claims "1B+ views that made him $6M+," and he quit banking in 2021. These are snippet-only and the dates are unclear. — [Denning: How I Made $150,000 From Writing in 30 Days](https://timdenning.substack.com/p/how-i-made-150000-from-writing-in); [Denning Substack profile](https://substack.com/@timdenning); [Writer's Hub: truth about writing on Medium](https://makemoneywriting.substack.com/p/so-heres-the-truth-about-writing)
- Denning's newsletter (Modern Freedom) reportedly passed 151,000 subscribers in Dec 2024. Source is promotional and unverified. — [yespress](https://yespress.io/tim-denning)
- Denning also published "You're Being Lied to About Medium Dot Com. You Can Make Plenty of Money There." Content not retrieved. — [Denning Substack](https://timdenning.substack.com/p/youre-being-lied-to-about-medium?action=share)
- **Kuegler:** left Medium in August 2023 for Substack. His Substack system was daily Notes, one free article a week, and 30 minutes a day commenting on articles he loved. He argues Medium rewards virality while Substack rewards reader relationships, and that "you can't build a relationship with your audience" on Medium. — [Newsletter Circle interview](https://newslettercircle.com/interviews/tom-kuegler-the-writing-long-game/); [The Writing Long Game](https://thewritinglonggame.substack.com/p/how-to-grow-your-substack-newsletter)
- **Zulie Rane:** Medium is "a vehicle to earn money by writing, not the destination." She points her newsletter and YouTube audiences at Medium stories ("if I have an existing audience… I can point eyeballs to my stories"). Most of her income comes from freelancing, with Medium building her network. — [Zulie Rane: transparent look at $1000/month](https://medium.com/@zulie_rane/a-transparent-look-at-how-i-make-1000-per-month-with-medium-ef388b692172); [Writerly Lifestyle Ep. 15](https://writerlylifestyle.com/zulierane/); [Become a Writer Today podcast](https://podcast.becomeawritertoday.com/1055644/episodes/8027030-getting-started-in-freelance-writing-with-zulie-rane)
- **Foroux:** does not rely on Medium for income. He built his own audience and digital products and reinvests earnings into writing. — [Foroux: 6 Steps to Get Rich as a Writer](https://dariusforoux.com/rich-writer/)
- **Towards Data Science:** independent from Feb 3, 2025. Medium began redirecting its custom domain at the end of January 2025, which hit traffic and SEO. TDS now pays contributors through its own "TDS Author Payment Program" and says "leaving Medium doesn't mean you have to leave Medium." It was acquired by Insight Media Group in 2024. — [TDS: Launching as an Independent Publication](https://towardsdatascience.com/towards-data-science-is-launching-as-an-independent-publication/); [AI Singapore repost](https://learn.aisingapore.org/2025/02/towards-data-science-is-launching-as-an-independent-publication/)
- Denning was cross-posting to Medium, LinkedIn and his own site at the same time. — [yespress](https://yespress.io/tim-denning)

### Inferences
- The canonical funnel is Medium (algorithmic and curated discovery, plus per-read income) → call to action for a newsletter (Substack, ConvertKit) → products, courses and coaching. After 2023 the biggest names moved their center of gravity to Substack and treat Medium as optional.
- Medium's 2025–26 changes (pay for search-engine discovery, pay for member conversions) look partly designed to keep writers who would otherwise send their traffic elsewhere.

### Gaps
- No documented X/LinkedIn repurposing workflows were found (e.g., thread → article) for these writers. Cole's Ship 30 for 30 / Typeshare atomic-essay method is known, but no 2023–26 source was retrieved here.
- No information on Niklas Göke's or Michael Thompson's funnels.

## 5. Earnings data and how platform changes shifted strategy

### Takeaway
Pay moved from claps (pre-2019) to member reading time (2019). The 2023 model (announced Aug, refined through Nov 2023) added **engagement signals (claps, highlights, replies, follows)** and a large role for **Boost**. Many veteran writers reported 25–90% drops. In 2025–26 Medium kept adjusting: extra pay for search-engine discovery (Oct 2025), Boost money spread to non-Boosted stories (Jan 2026), conversion rewards (Feb 17, 2026), and editor pay (Jun 1, 2026). Reads by non-members still pay nothing.

### Cited Findings
- 2019: Medium moved Partner Program pay from claps to members' reading time. — [TechCrunch, Oct 22 2019](https://techcrunch.com/2019/10/22/medium-reading-time/)
- 2023 model: member reading and listening time stays the primary factor, and claps, highlights, replies and follows also contribute. "The more time members spend reading or listening to your story, the more you earn." — [Medium Help: earnings calculation](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036691193-Calculating-earnings-in-the-Partner-Program); [Medium Blog: Evolving the Partner Program](https://blog.medium.com/evolving-the-partner-program-2613708f9f3c); [Medium: Improving how we calculate writer earnings (via Axios link)](https://medium.com/@emmaa/d2d3f4329b26)
- A member read counts only after 30+ seconds, and idle time is excluded. Boosted stories earn engagement points at a higher rate. These come from third-party guides and are unofficial. — [Write a Catalyst: How the MPP really works](https://medium.com/write-a-catalyst/how-the-medium-partner-program-really-works-and-how-readers-quietly-control-a-writers-income-251b19a7ce10)
- Writer reaction to the 2023 change: earnings fell "anywhere from 25 to 90 percent." This is an individual account. — [Medium post on new model (via search)](https://medium.com/blogging-guide/mediums-new-method-of-calculating-earnings-965d5dc2dcff); a 2023 critique says the new scheme hurts quality writers through lower read-through, less engagement and fewer Boosts — [M.
